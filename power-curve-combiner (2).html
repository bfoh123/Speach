Create new
Add the curve from a screenshot, using the name, colour and group above. Accepts PNG, JPG and HEIC. Click along the curve to trace it. Your trace is drawn in pink so you can check it.
Click a button, then click that gridline or label position on the image, and type its value beside it.
Generate a crew curve Choose a group, tick the athletes you want, then press Make crew curve to put their saved curves on the graph. Ticks are kept when you switch group.
Generate an average curve (athlete) Tick at least three curves on the graph. The average curve is the mean of their power.
Generate an average curve (crew) Averages every curve currently on the active graph into one curve. You need at least two curves on the graph.
Find the most equal curves (boat) Finds the group of rowers whose power curves are most alike, so they can go in a boat together.
